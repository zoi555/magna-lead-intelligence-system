A · Contour Line Wordmark

geographic · calm · territory
Primary · light
Primary · dark (white-on-dark)
Compact (sidebar/header)
Single-colour (mono ink) · white-on-dark · favicon

Why it works: the contour reads as a territory boundary / map — instantly says “geographic intelligence,” stays calm and premium. Risks: the line can look decorative or get lost at tiny sizes; keep it subtle and drop it below ~16px height.

B · Perspective Cut Wordmark

sharp · premium · likely front-runner
Primary · light
Primary · dark (white-on-dark)
Compact (sidebar/header)
Single-colour · white-on-dark · favicon

Why it works: the skew + facet cut expresses “aspect / viewpoint / angle” inside the type itself — sharp, modern, ownable, distinctly SaaS. The favicon derives from the facet, not an “A”. Risks: skew must stay subtle (≤7°) to keep legibility; a heavier cut could read like an italic gimmick — needs a real type-designer pass to refine the letterforms.

C · Route Stroke Wordmark

discovery · movement · signal
Primary · light
Primary · dark (white-on-dark)
Compact (sidebar/header)
Single-colour · white-on-dark · favicon

Why it works: the route + signal node reads as opportunity discovery, sales movement, and territory expansion; the cyan end-node = a live signal. Risks: a route/path can drift toward a delivery/logistics look — keep it abstract (node-dots, not trucks/arrows) and never add a pin or vehicle.
